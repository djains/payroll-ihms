## 1. Introduction and Scope

**NG Global** (the "App") is an enterprise workforce management, biometric attendance verification, and automated payroll application provided by **NG Global** ("we", "us", or "our").

The App is designed exclusively for employees, contractual workforce personnel, supervisors, and authorised staff of organisations utilizing NG Global enterprise services (each an **"Employer"**).

### Data Fiduciary vs. Data Processor Roles

Under applicable data protection laws, including the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**:
* **The Employer as Data Fiduciary:** For all employment, biometric verification, attendance muster, and payroll information, the Employer determines the purposes and means of processing personal data and acts as the **Data Fiduciary**.
* **NG Global as Data Processor:** NG Global processes such employment and attendance information strictly on behalf of the Employer as a **Data Processor**, subject to applicable law, service level agreements, and contractual instructions.

This Privacy Policy describes our data practices in full accordance with applicable Indian data protection and information technology laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable Google Play User Data policies.

## 3. App Permissions and Their Purpose

The App requests only necessary Android permissions required for legitimate workplace functions in full compliance with **Google Play Prominent Disclosure** guidelines:

### 📍 Location Permission (Foreground & Background Access)
* **Foreground Location:** Accessed when the employee taps "Punch In", "Punch Out", or "Break" to ensure they are physically inside the Employer's authorized geofenced premises.
* **Background Location (Role-Specific):** For authorized field employees, security guards, delivery personnel, or patrolling supervisors where background tracking is explicitly configured by the Employer, location coordinates are processed strictly during **active scheduled shift hours**.
* **Zero Commercial Selling:** Location coordinates are transmitted solely to the Employer's enterprise database over encrypted TLS connections. **Location data is NEVER sold, rented, leased, or shared with third-party advertisers, ad networks, or data brokers.**

### 📷 Camera Permission
Camera access is required exclusively for:
1. Capturing a live attendance selfie at the moment of Punch-In and Punch-Out.
2. Verifying worker identity during initial profile enrollment by supervisors.
3. Attaching verified site photos where required for field reporting.

*Camera access is user-initiated. The App does not operate the camera in the background or access stored gallery photos for attendance verification.*

### 📁 Storage and Document Access (Scoped Storage)
In full compliance with Android Scoped Storage:
1. **Downloading Salary Slips:** Saving monthly PDF salary slips and tax statements directly to the device's public Download directory for employee reference.
2. **Uploading KYC Documents:** Allowing authorized users to select and upload necessary employment documents (e.g., PAN card, bank passbook) during initial staff onboarding.

*The App accesses only files specifically selected or generated by the user and never scans or accesses unrelated personal files or media.*

### 📱 Device Information & Hardware Binding
Limited device parameters (Device Model, Android OS Version, unique hardware identifier) are processed solely for:
* Hardware device binding to ensure an employee account cannot be fraudulently accessed from unrecognized or unauthorized devices.
* Account authentication security and session verification.

*Device information is never accessed or used for advertising, tracking, or marketing purposes.*

## 4. How We Use and Share Information

### Permitted Uses of Information
Personal and workplace information is processed strictly for:

* Geofence-verified attendance recording and shift scheduling.
* Break tracking, overtime calculation, and duty hour computation.

2. **Authorized Service Providers:** Cloud infrastructure providers, secure SMS/email dispatch gateways, and technical hosting partners operating under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
3. **Statutory and Legal Authorities:** Disclosures mandated by applicable Indian law, court subpoenas, statutory labor audits (e.g., Minimum Wages Act, Factories Act), or regulatory enforcement orders.

## 5. Data Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect workforce data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, destruction, or disclosure:

* **Encryption in Transit:** All network communication between the mobile application and backend enterprise servers is protected using industry-standard **HTTPS / 256-bit TLS (Transport Layer Security)** encryption.
* **Secure Credential Storage:** User authentication tokens and session keys are secured using native Android **EncryptedSharedPreferences / Hardware Keystore** mechanisms.
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict administrative boundaries ensure employees and supervisors can only view information authorized for their operational scope.
* **Database Protection:** Hosted within secured tier-4 enterprise data centers with automated intrusion detection, automated backups, and firewall protection.
* **Data Breach Response:** In the unlikely event of a security incident affecting personal data, NG Global will promptly notify affected Employers and relevant regulatory authorities in compliance with applicable laws.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

### Data Retention Periods
* **Operational Retention:** Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the workforce management purposes for which it was collected.
* **Statutory Compliance Retention:** Under Indian labor and corporate regulations (including the Payment of Wages Act, Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, and Income Tax Act), historical payroll muster, tax deduction summaries, and attendance logs must be retained for statutory audit periods (typically 5 to 8 years) by the Employer.
* **Selfie Photographs & Shift Coordinates:** Raw attendance selfies and GPS coordinates are archived or purged in accordance with the Employer's documented record-retention schedule.

### Account and Data Deletion Requests
* Employees seeking deletion of their account or personal data can submit an official request through their Employer's HR department or by emailing **`support@ngglobal.in`**.
* Following identity verification, non-statutory data (such as login tokens, profile photographs, and mobile app access) will be deactivated and removed within **30 business days**.
* Legally mandated financial, wage disbursement, and statutory tax records will be retained in accordance with applicable statutory mandates.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and applicable privacy laws, data principals (employees and users) possess the following rights:

1. **Right to Access & Summary:** The right to request confirmation and a summary of personal data being processed by the platform.
2. **Right to Correction & Completion:** The right to request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal records.
3. **Right to Erasure:** The right to request deletion of personal information no longer required for legitimate employment or statutory purposes.
4. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** The right to have data grievances resolved through a designated Grievance Officer within established timeframes.
5. **Right to Nominate:** The right to nominate an individual to exercise privacy rights on their behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
6. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to operational employment implications.

*To exercise any of these rights, users may contact their organization's HR department or reach out directly to our Grievance Redressal Cell at the contact below.*
